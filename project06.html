Project 6: Simple Car

This was our first project focused more on software than on the car's physical mechanics. Building the car was straightforward; the main challenge was programming it to complete a set of movement tasks:

  • Drive forward at slow and fast speeds
  • Drive backward at slow and fast speeds
  • Spin clockwise and counterclockwise
  • Drive along small-radius and large-radius arcs

This was our first time coding the LEGO Spike hubs. It was cool to experiment with the software and see the different capabilities it offered.
