Project 11: High or Low

This project challenged me to create a supervised classification device that can identify “high” versus “low” heights using a trained model. The program has two modes:

  1. Training (Learning) Mode: The hub reads and stores pairs of distance sensor readings and motor positions.
  2. Inference (Prediction) Mode: For a new distance sensor reading, the program finds the nearest training samples and uses their motor positions to make a prediction.

In Training Mode, the hub displayed “TRAIN” on its light matrix. As I raised and lowered the hub, I pressed the left button to record a distance reading and its matching motor position for a height I considered “high” or “low.” Pressing the right button ended training and started Prediction Mode. I could then raise the hub to a height, and it used the closest recorded samples to predict whether the height was high or low.
